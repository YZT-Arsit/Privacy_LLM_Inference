\section{Operator Construction}
\label{app:operators}

\subsection{Linear Boundary}

For $\X\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times\dinX}$,
$\W\in\mathbb{R}^{\dinX\times\doutX}$, learned
$\bias\in\mathbb{R}^{\doutX}$, invertible
$\Nin\in\mathbb{R}^{\dinX\times\dinX}$ and
$\Nout\in\mathbb{R}^{\doutX\times\doutX}$, and
$\Tpad\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times\dinX}$, the TEE constructs
\begin{align}
\Xt&=(\X-\Tpad)\Nin, &
\Wt&=\Nin^{-1}\W\Nout,\\
\Ct&=\Tpad\W\Nout, &
\Yt&=\Xt\Wt+\bias\Nout+\Ct.
\label{eq:app-linear}
\end{align}
The GPU evaluates the last expression and the TEE applies
$\Recover(\Yt)=\Yt\Nout^{-1}$. A broadcast implementation samples the masked
pad $\Tpad\Nin\in\mathbb{R}^{\dinX}$ directly and broadcasts its
$\doutX$-dimensional compensation. Pads terminate at the linear boundary and
do not enter RoPE, softmax, normalization, or pointwise activation cores.

\subsection{Attention, RoPE, and Cache}

For each head, the TEE applies RoPE to recovered $\Qmat$ and $\K$, then forms
$\Qt=\RoPE(\Qmat)\NQ$, $\Kt=\RoPE(\K)\NK$, and
$\Vt=\Vmat\NV$, with
\begin{equation}
\NQ\NK^{\mathsf T}=\mathbf I_{d_h}.
\label{eq:app-attention-compat}
\end{equation}
For GQA, each group of $h/h_{kv}$ query heads derives its $\NQ$ from the
shared key head's $\NK$; MQA is $h_{kv}=1$. No mask mixes heads. The output
projection folds the appropriate per-head $\NV^{-1}$ into its input basis.

During prefill, each layer stores $\Kt,\Vt$ with per-head shape
$n\times d_h$. At decode position $t$, the GPU appends one
$1\times d_h$ row and evaluates a $1\times(t+1)$ score row. Because $\NK$ and
$\NV$ are fixed within the session, token-axis concatenation yields
$[\K_1\NK;\ldots;\K_t\NK]=[\K_1;\ldots;\K_t]\NK$, and analogously for
values. The wired theorem target masks after RoPE; pre-RoPE commutation is used
only by variants whose masks are restricted to RoPE-compatible two-dimensional
blocks.

\subsection{Normalization, Residuals, and Activations}

The canonical path executes embedding lookup, RMSNorm including private gain,
final normalization, RoPE, recovery, residual addition, logit recovery, token
selection, loss evaluation, and derivatives of trusted operators in the TEE.
The GPU evaluates attention softmax/value aggregation and operator-compatible
pointwise activations. For SwiGLU, gate and up projections share one channel
permutation $\Perm$; the down projection folds $\Perm^{-1}$. GELU and ReLU use
the same permutation boundary. A transformed residual update returns to the
TEE before residual addition.

\subsection{Rank Padding and LoRA Forward}

The User-owned factors are
$\Amat\in\mathbb{R}^{\dinX\times\rtrue}$ and
$\Bmat\in\mathbb{R}^{\rtrue\times\doutX}$. The TEE constructs
$\Apad\in\mathbb{R}^{\dinX\times\rpad}$ and
$\Bpad\in\mathbb{R}^{\rpad\times\doutX}$ such that
$\Apad\Bpad=\Amat\Bmat$. Exact-cancellation padding uses either a random down
block paired with a zero up block or paired columns/rows with opposite
contributions. Scaling remains $\alpha/\rtrue$.

For a call-local $\Umix\in\mathbb{R}^{\rpad\times\rpad}$,
\begin{align}
\At&=\Nin^{-1}\Apad\Umix,&
\Bt&=\Umix^{-1}\Bpad\Nout.
\label{eq:app-lora-factors}
\end{align}
The GPU keeps the base and adapter branches separate and evaluates
\begin{multline}
\Yt=\Xt\Wt+\frac{\alpha}{\rtrue}(\Xt\At)\Bt+\bias\Nout+\Ct\\
+\frac{\alpha}{\rtrue}\Tpad\Apad\Bpad\Nout.
\label{eq:app-lora-forward}
\end{multline}

\subsection{LoRA Backward and Optimizer Interface}

Given $\Gmat\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times\doutX}$, the TEE sends
$\Gt=\Gmat\Nout^{-\mathsf T}$. The GPU computes
\begin{align}
d\At&=\frac{\alpha}{\rtrue}\Xt^{\mathsf T}
       (\Gt\Bt^{\mathsf T}),&
d\Bt&=\frac{\alpha}{\rtrue}(\Xt\At)^{\mathsf T}\Gt.
\label{eq:app-lora-backward}
\end{align}
The TEE recovers padded-coordinate gradients by
\begin{align}
d\Apad&=\Nin^{-\mathsf T}d\At\Umix^{\mathsf T},&
d\Bpad&=\Umix^{-\mathsf T}d\Bt\Nout^{\mathsf T},
\label{eq:app-lora-gradient-recovery}
\end{align}
and, when a pad is used, adds
\begin{align}
d\Apad&\mathrel{+}=\frac{\alpha}{\rtrue}
 \Tpad^{\mathsf T}\Gmat\Bpad^{\mathsf T},\\
d\Bpad&\mathrel{+}=\frac{\alpha}{\rtrue}
 \Apad^{\mathsf T}\Tpad^{\mathsf T}\Gmat.
\label{eq:app-lora-gradient-pad}
\end{align}
The first $\rtrue$ columns/rows give $d\Amat,d\Bmat$. The TEE owns the loss,
real-rank gradients, factors, and optimizer state, performs the update, and
constructs fresh transformed factors for the next call. Full nonlinear masked
backward is not assumed; trusted derivatives provide the upstream gradient at
each delegated linear boundary.
